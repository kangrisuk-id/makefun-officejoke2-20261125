# Candidates — One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

Forty original one-line office jokes, each scored against the judge's five
published criteria, in the judge's own order:

1. **On-brief** — really a one-line office joke about work life
2. **Laugh** — lands a real laugh; sharp, surprising punchline, not a mild observation
3. **Instant** — clear on one read, no setup that needs explaining
4. **Fresh** — avoids the office clichés everyone has heard before
5. **Tight** — every word earns its place

Each criterion is scored 1–5, for a maximum of 25. The top eight seeds then
played a single-elimination knockout; head-to-head ties break on criterion 2
(the laugh), then criterion 4 (freshness), exactly as the judge's brief says
to walk the criteria in order.

## Knockout — top 8 seeds

Seeds: 1) #23 (24), 2) #13 (24), 3) #30 (23), 4) #31 (22), 5) #17 (22),
6) #3 (22), 7) #33 (22), 8) #1 (22). The eleven other 22-point lines lost the
last seeds on criterion 4 (freshness) or criterion 5 (tightness).

### Quarter-finals

- **#23 beat #1** — the shredder's double punch (a real shredder *and* the
  idiom) lands a bigger laugh than the laptop's sick day.
- **#30 beat #31** — one word of punchline ("again.") beats a definition joke
  on tightness and freshness.
- **#13 beat #33** — the recursive out-of-office reply is fresher than the
  stapler's remote-work gag, a joke the whole field is telling this year.
- **#3 beat #17** — the fire-exit point is a visual gag that lands instantly;
  "strategic ambiguity" needs a beat to unpack.

### Semi-finals

- **#23 beat #30** — both are tight and fresh; the shredder wins on
  criterion 2, a full laugh against a wry smile.
- **#13 beat #3** — equal laughs, but the meeting-about-this-email loop is
  the fresher mechanism.

### Final

- **#23 beat #13, 24/25 vs 24/25** — level on total, so the judge's own
  order decides: criterion 2, the laugh. #13 is clever; #23 is clever *and*
  detonates — "cut out the middleman" turns a dull office ritual into a
  confession the whole office recognizes. The shredder takes the crown.

## Winner

> We replaced the suggestion box with a shredder to cut out the middleman.
